Terms

Playing on tapmelo

You can play the games on tapmelo for free in your browser without installing anything or creating a tapmelo account.

Glass Twist rewards

In Glass Twist, optional rewards are free. Confirm the prompt to get the extra box, temporary slot or melt it describes. Closing the prompt gives you no reward. Extra boxes and slots last for the current round, and you can use melt once per round. You can restart any level for free. Restarting clears that round’s rewards.

Saved progress

tapmelo’s own games save progress in this browser when storage is available. The games save different things, and progress doesn’t sync across devices. Clearing site data removes these saves. The Privacy page explains what each game saves, how analytics works and how to turn it off.

Using the site

You may browse and play for personal use. Please don’t interfere with the site or pass off its content as your own.
