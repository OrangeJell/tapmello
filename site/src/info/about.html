About tapmelo

Find a game

Browse all games by category or search by name. You can play puzzles, arcade games, racing games and more for free in your browser. There’s nothing to install and no tapmelo account to create.

Open Played games to find games you’ve started in this browser. Use the language menu to read the site in your preferred language. Language options inside each game vary.

Games from tapmelo

Match office workers to shuttle buses in Clock Out!, sort screws from layers of glass in Glass Twist, or learn tile combinations and practise reading hands in Mahjong. Each has guides on its game page.

The catalogue also includes games from 1Games and Playgama. The game loads after you choose Play now on its page.
