Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

What this browser saves

Glass Twist saves your completed levels, settings and unfinished puzzle, including any rewards you’ve used or claimed. Mahjong saves lesson progress and preferences. Clock Out! saves your highest unlocked level and sound setting. Reloading Clock Out! starts a fresh round.

When you start a game, tapmelo saves its ID and when you last launched it. That’s how Continue playing and Played games know what to show. The site also saves when to show bookmark or home-screen reminders, whether you’ve turned them off and, when your browser reports it, whether you’ve installed tapmelo. Glass Twist keeps a short play timer in your current tab for these reminders.

These records stay in your browser. Clearing site data removes them. On iPhone and iPad, a home-screen shortcut may keep its own game progress. The shortcut still needs an internet connection. Glass Twist can play sound and vibrate on supported devices. You can turn both off from the pause menu.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 runs when you open tapmelo unless you’ve turned it off in this browser. There’s no consent prompt. Google may use cookies such as _ga and process information such as your device, browser and approximate location. Jelly uses the reports to see where visits come from, which pages people use and how Glass Twist runs.

Glass Twist sends events for things like loading the game, the level you played, successful move counts, time spent playing, wins, jams and confirmed restarts. The analytics tag also counts interactions with bookmark and home-screen reminders. Tapping “I’ve added it” is recorded separately from installations reported by the browser.

These events use temporary page, round and event IDs. The site doesn’t save those IDs after you leave the page or send your name, email, saved game file or individual screw choices in its custom events.

See how Google uses information from sites like tapmelo and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Turn analytics on or off

This choice applies to this browser after the page reloads. Turning analytics off stops new collection by tapmelo’s Google Analytics tag. Data already sent to Google and existing cookies stay in place. Clearing site data resets the choice. You can still play with analytics off.

Checking your analytics setting…

You can also use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on in supported browsers.

Playgama games

When you press Play on a game from Playgama, your browser connects to Playgama or the game’s host. The game may use cookies or similar storage. For details, see Playgama’s Privacy Policy.

Hosting and external links

Cloudflare serves tapmelo and may process technical details such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site. The homepage also loads a badge image from Fazier, so your browser sends a request to Fazier. When you follow an external link, the site you open has its own privacy policy.
